Meet Tiara

A thoughtful eye for real moments.

Tiara is drawn to the honest details that make a story feel personal: the way someone talks about work they care about, the energy of a room, or the moment an audience truly connects.

From a simple testimonial to a promotional film or live event, she brings a calm, thoughtful approach to filming and editing—keeping the process clear and the finished piece true to the people in it.
